Composition notes

Open positions at the selected price mark, weighted by market value; sold positions and dividend cash are excluded. Today's quote-based snapshot is provisional. Current sector classifications are used for every snapshot. Funds / ETFs are grouped separately, without looking through their holdings.

For each security, total P&L is position value plus sale proceeds and dividends minus all buy costs through the snapshot. Return on buys is P&L divided by those buy costs. These figures include closed trades. Average buy and sell prices cover all trades on each side through that date, on the snapshot's split-adjusted share basis; they are not the cost basis of the remaining shares. Performance charts use time-weighted returns over their own selected period.

Chart notes

Range buttons update every performance chart and risk metric. Quarterly returns compound the same time-weighted series, retaining dividends as cash. The first price mark is the baseline; first and last quarterly bars may cover part of a quarter. Today's endpoint is provisional when it uses regular-session quotes. Risk metrics use completed daily returns. Comparisons use matching benchmark dates. Trade arrows link to transactions. The total-return benchmark reinvests dividends.
