How to read this projection

Success means the plan covers every monthly retirement withdrawal through your chosen end age and meets any final balance goal. Income above spending is invested at the start of each retirement month.

The model samples independent monthly market returns using fixed assumptions. Savings are added at month end before retirement; retirement withdrawals come out at month start. Fees and inflation are applied monthly. Savings, spending, and other income retain constant purchasing power.

This is a hypothetical planning tool, not a forecast or investment recommendation. Taxes, debt, changing asset allocation, and unexpected expenses are not modeled. Extreme market outcomes can differ from these simulations. The same inputs use the same random sample so scenarios are easier to compare.
