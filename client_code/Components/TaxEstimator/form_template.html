Coverage, assumptions & sources

This planner uses year-specific 2025 or 2026 US federal brackets, short/long capital-gain netting, the annual capital-loss deduction and the 3.8% net investment income tax. Amounts are in USD. It does not submit returns or execute trades. Save inputs stores your tax-year assumptions and hypothetical trades privately with this portfolio, so you can continue across visits. Synced holdings and recorded sales load automatically. Unsaved draft changes stay in this page while you navigate.

State/local amounts use the marginal rates you enter, with flat-rate presets for Colorado, Florida, Illinois and Pennsylvania. State deductions, credits, annual exclusions and state-specific loss rules are not calculated. Washington's capital-gains excise tax is not supported. Federal only explicitly excludes state/local tax. Other countries, part-year/multiple-state residency, AMT, collectibles, options, retirement-account withdrawals, tax credits, income-dependent deductions and future corporate actions are outside this estimate.

The latest synced snapshot supplies FIFO lots, split adjustments, current quotes, and recorded annual realized gains without another price request. Prior-year views use year-end quotes. Synced dividends accrue on ex-date; confirm payment timing before including them, and enter any qualified portion from broker records. Tax income fields exclude the synced dividends, which are added automatically when confirmed. Include other gains and loss carryforwards only in the additional fields to avoid double counting. Tax-loss carryforward estimates are simplified. Missing historical prices use market estimates when available. Wash-sale flags are conservative; no replacement-lot basis adjustment is automatic. Verify inputs against broker records and review the result with a tax professional.
