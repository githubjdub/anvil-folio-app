Coverage, assumptions & sources

This planner uses 2026 US federal brackets, short/long capital-gain netting, the annual capital-loss deduction and the 3.8% net investment income tax. Amounts are in USD. It does not submit returns or execute trades. Scenarios stay in this page while you navigate and are cleared when you change accounts or portfolios or reload.

State/local amounts use the marginal rates you enter, with flat-rate presets for Colorado, Florida, Illinois and Pennsylvania. State deductions, credits, annual exclusions and state-specific loss rules are not calculated. Washington's capital-gains excise tax is not supported. Federal only explicitly excludes state/local tax. Other countries, part-year/multiple-state residency, AMT, collectibles, options, retirement-account withdrawals, tax credits, income-dependent deductions and future corporate actions are outside this estimate.

Logged transactions through the selected snapshot supply the initial lots and annual realized gains. Include other gains and loss carryforwards only in the additional fields to avoid double counting. Tax-loss carryforward estimates are simplified. Missing historical prices use market estimates when available. Wash-sale flags are conservative; no replacement-lot basis adjustment is automatic. Verify inputs against broker records and review the result with a tax professional.
