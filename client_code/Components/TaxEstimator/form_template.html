How this estimate works & sources

The estimate uses the latest saved sync for this portfolio: recorded sales, FIFO cost basis, split adjustments, and accrued dividends. “This year’s portfolio activity” excludes unrealized gains and is not an annual forecast. “Sell all current holdings” adds hypothetical sales at synced prices as of the snapshot (or year end for a prior tax year). It does not place trades or fetch new prices. Unsynced edits are excluded.

Quick estimates assume US taxable stock and ETF accounts, the basic standard deduction, no outside investment income or capital-loss carryforwards, and the filing status shown above. Blank annual income means $0 other income; add approximate household income before tax for a more useful bracket estimate. Income is treated as modified AGI before portfolio activity. Itemized deductions, dependents, additional age/blindness deductions, credits, retirement accounts, AMT, collectibles and non-US taxes are outside this estimate. Detailed inputs override the quick income assumptions.

Accrued dividends are included by default as ordinary dividends assumed paid in the selected year. Ex-date does not establish payment date or qualified status; exclude or adjust these in optional details using broker records. State/local tax is excluded by default; optional rates are marginal planning estimates, with flat-rate presets for Colorado, Florida, Illinois and Pennsylvania. Washington capital-gains excise tax is not supported.

The headline subtracts estimated tax without this portfolio from estimated tax with it, including dividends, recorded gains/losses, and hypothetical trades. After-tax portfolio income is realized gains/losses plus included dividends minus that tax. Hypothetical after-tax cash subtracts only the additional tax caused by the hypothetical trades from their net cash proceeds. Loss savings and carryforwards are simplified. Possible wash-sale losses require review; basis adjustments are not automatic. Compare estimates with broker records. Preferences and trade drafts are saved privately per portfolio and tax year after a successful estimate.
