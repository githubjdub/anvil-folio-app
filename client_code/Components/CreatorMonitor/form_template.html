Timing starts with this feature and is approximate: only visible, focused tabs, with a two-minute idle cutoff and 30-second updates. Unfinished intervals and disconnected visits can be missed. Community holdings reference

Most owned stocks & funds

Uses one portfolio per account: the most recently saved. This avoids counting multiple saved scenarios as extra wealth. Positions reflect each portfolio's saved cutoff, marked at the latest available price; these are saved scenarios, not connected brokerage balances.

Prices: Yahoo Finance regular-session marks or latest completed closes; each row shows its price date. Rankings include priced positions only. Entered cost uses FIFO remaining lots and recorded purchase prices, with split adjustments; missing purchase prices remain unknown. Cost coverage is the share of open quantity with entered purchase prices. Fees, taxes, cash and private assets are excluded.
