Rules known at the time

Compare each completed daily close with the preceding lookback window, excluding that day. A qualifying close schedules a trade at the next session's open; it does not buy or sell at an extreme learned afterward. Opening gaps can change the execution price. Daily data cannot locate the exact second of a price touch.

Allocate capital equally across the filtered ticker list. Buy once per stock; sell once at the first qualifying exit, then hold any remainder. Unused capital and sale proceeds stay in cash. Dividends accrue on ex-dates as receivables, with no reinvestment.

Assumptions & data notes

Daily equity includes open positions, cash, and accrued dividend entitlement. Buy-and-hold comparisons buy at the first test session's open and use the same dividend setting. Ineligible stocks with insufficient warmup remain in cash in the same-stock comparison. Figures exclude fees, taxes, and slippage; historical results are hypothetical.
