How this comparison works

Both scenarios repeat the quantities and prices of your logged buys and sells. Sale proceeds stay in cash. The reinvestment scenario buys fractional shares of the paying security; those extra shares remain invested even after your original shares are sold. If a position is fully closed when reinvestment would occur, its payment stays in cash.

Dividend entitlement uses shares held before the ex-dividend date. Earned dividends are included as receivables until payment. Reinvestment uses the payment date's completed closing price, or the next available close, so it may differ from your broker's actual DRIP execution.

These are hypothetical scenarios based on historical data and the payment dates you review. They exclude taxes, trading fees, and cash interest. Your entered transactions and saved portfolio are not changed.
